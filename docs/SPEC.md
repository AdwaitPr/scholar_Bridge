# ScholarBridge: Technical Architecture, Deterministic Eligibility Engine, and Product Specification for Student Scholarship Discovery and Application Management

## Executive Summary and Ecosystem Context

Fragmentation across educational funding portals creates structural barriers that prevent eligible students from discovering, preparing for, and securing financial aid. Within the Indian higher and school education landscape during the 2026–27 academic session, scholarship administration spans centralized federal platforms such as the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) and myscheme.gov.in, autonomous statutory bodies including the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), state-level e-District welfare portals, and private philanthropic foundations. While federal initiatives have consolidated disbursement through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) linked to Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts and introduced One Time Registration (OTR) via Aadhaar or Enrollment IDs (EID) to eliminate repetitive annual registration, students still confront severe informational asymmetries.

A primary driver of application failure is the divergence between third-party educational aggregators and authoritative government notifications. During the 2026–27 cycle, secondary portals frequently reported static closing dates of October 31, 2026, or listed major UGC schemes as unopened, whereas the live authoritative feed on scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships extended student application windows for AICTE Pragati, Saksham, Swanath, UGC Ishan Uday, and the National Scholarship for Post Graduate Studies (NSPGS) through November 30, 2026, with defective application and institute-level verification continuing through December 15, 2026. Conversely, schemes such as the PM-USP Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship (CSSS) opened exclusively for renewals between June 1, 2026, and October 31, 2026, leaving fresh first-year applicants waiting for separate board-percentile releases. Furthermore, regulatory updates introduced on the National Scholarship Portal for the 2026–27 academic year permit students to apply for one merit-based scholarship scheme alongside one or more welfare-based scholarship schemes, even though final disbursement rules strictly prohibit holding concurrent central or state scholarships simultaneously.

ScholarBridge resolves these structural bottlenecks by replacing static directory listings with a deterministic, source-verified discovery and application lifecycle platform. Rather than acting as an unverified aggregator or relying on probabilistic generative models that risk fabricating eligibility thresholds or deadlines, ScholarBridge implements an explainable Boolean and relational rules engine backed by a strict source verification register. The platform guides students across a continuous six-stage operational pipeline encompassing structured profile onboarding with strict data minimization, multi-faceted scholarship discovery, deterministic eligibility evaluation with clause-level explanations, dynamic document checklist generation, multi-state application preparation tracking, and time-zone-aware deadline management.

## System Architecture and Modular Engineering Blueprint

ScholarBridge utilizes a unified, type-safe full-stack architecture built on the Next.js App Router with TypeScript, styled via Tailwind CSS and accessible shadcn/ui primitives, and backed by Supabase PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security (RLS) and Supabase Auth. This architecture eliminates the operational overhead of maintaining separate microservice clusters while enforcing strict boundary separation between public catalog queries and isolated student application records.

### Domain Module Decomposition

The backend and frontend codebases are organized into eight cohesive domain modules located under `src/modules/`, each encapsulating its own Zod validation schemas, database access repositories, business logic services, and UI components.

1. **student-profile**: Manages minimal demographic, academic, course, state domicile, and income bracket attributes required for matching. No Aadhaar, PAN, or bank account numbers stored.
2. **scholarship-catalog**: Stores core scholarship records, benefit structures, required document templates, and authoritative URLs. Publicly readable for active records; write-restricted to admin role.
3. **scholarship-discovery**: Executes full-text search, multi-attribute filtering, and pagination. Publicly accessible; enriches results with eligibility states when an authenticated profile exists.
4. **eligibility-engine**: Evaluates student profile attributes against structured AST rule trees. Stateless, pure deterministic TypeScript execution.
5. **application-tracker**: Manages saved scholarships and transitions applications across the 9 lifecycle states (Saved through Closed). Strict RLS isolation (auth.uid() = user_id).
6. **checklist-manager**: Instantiates scholarship-specific document requirements into trackable student checklists. Strict RLS isolation; metadata only, no document files.
7. **deadline-management**: Computes time-remaining intervals in Asia/Kolkata (UTC+05:30), flags expired schemes, distinguishes student vs institute verification dates, and generates in-app reminders.
8. **scholarship-administration**: Provides authorized curators with CRUD interfaces, source URL verification workflows, deadline updates, and immutable audit logging.
